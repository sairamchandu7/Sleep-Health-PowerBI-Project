# 💤 Sleep Health & Lifestyle Dashboard


## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sleep Health & Lifestyle Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze how lifestyle factors influence sleep quality and overall health.

This dashboard enables users to explore patterns in sleep duration, stress levels, physical activity, and BMI through dynamic visualizations.



## ❓ Problem Statement

Poor sleep quality is often influenced by multiple lifestyle factors such as stress level, physical activity, occupation, and BMI. Organizations and health professionals require clear, data-driven insights to understand these relationships.

This project aims to analyze sleep health data and build an interactive Power BI dashboard that highlights key patterns affecting sleep quality and duration.



## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze sleep duration and quality trends
* Identify lifestyle factors affecting sleep
* Compare stress levels vs sleep patterns
* Provide actionable health insights through visualization

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Individuals with higher physical activity tend to have better sleep quality
* Increased stress levels correlate with reduced sleep duration
* BMI category shows noticeable variation across age groups
* Sleep disorders are more common in high-stress segments
  


## 📐 Key DAX Measures

The following DAX calculations were implemented to support advanced analytics:

* **Average Sleep Duration** — calculates mean sleep hours
* **Sleep Quality Index** — evaluates overall sleep performance
* **Stress Level Category** — segments users by stress intensity
* **BMI Category Distribution** — analyzes body mass trends
